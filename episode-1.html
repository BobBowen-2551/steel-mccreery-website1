County Boys

Doc Stone returns to The County after time away. The bridge, gravel roads and familiar shoreline bring back the rhythms of home.

The journey home

The first chapter follows Ol’ Doc Stone as he crosses the bridge into The County. The pace of life changes as familiar roads and landmarks come back into view. A place can change while still holding the people and memories that made it home.

The Watering Hole is waiting. It is more than a bar: it is where people who have left town discover that they still belong. Doc’s return establishes the emotional centre of the season.
