Season One · Episode 04

Drinks With Friends

Jimmy brings Whiskey Jack to see Doc, hoping to pass along some of the kindness he received the night before.

Passing it forward

Jimmy has been thinking about Doc’s advice. He goes to Iron Creek Tractor Co. to collect Whiskey Jack, believing a night with good company might do Jack some good. They head together to The Watering Hole.

Hank McCoy, Jack’s brother, serves drinks and watches the familiar scene unfold. Jack keeps the jokes and whiskey coming; Doc and Jimmy exchange a knowing look. Eventually Jimmy drives Jack home, where Jenny is waiting. The night winds down in the way County nights often do: reluctantly.

THEMESFriendship, loyalty and looking out for one another
