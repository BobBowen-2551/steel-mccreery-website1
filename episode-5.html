Whiskey Jack

Meet the tractor man whose big reputation hides a generous heart—and whose life changes when Jenny Dunn enters it.

The man behind the stories

Whiskey Jack McCoy is a local character with a reputation for whiskey, wild nights and an unstoppable appetite for fun. Yet his friends know a second side: he is dependable, hard-working and quick to help someone in need.

Jack has loved tractors since childhood and built a life around Iron Creek Tractor Co. His relationship with Jenny Dunn adds another dimension to a man many people thought they already understood.
