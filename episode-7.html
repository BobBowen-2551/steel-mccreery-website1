Martha Died On A Tuesday

Doc Stone’s past comes into focus through his grief for Martha and the memories he carries into every June.

The story Doc carries

The final chapter of the first season reveals a quieter side of Ol’ Doc Stone. The man who offers advice to everyone else has a sorrow of his own: the loss of Martha.

Every June 6, memories of Martha return with particular force. This episode gives new meaning to Doc’s kindness and to the moments when he urges younger people not to take their loved ones for granted.

THEMESLove, grief and remembrance
