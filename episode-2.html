The Watering Hole

Jimmy Dunn finds his way back to the County’s oldest bar, where familiar faces and music offer him a moment to breathe.

A night back in town

Jimmy Dunn has been away from The County for a long time. With responsibilities and trouble at home weighing on him, he returns to the bar where so many local stories begin.

The Watering Hole sits beside the beach. Hank McCoy tends bar, Doc keeps an eye on the room, and Whiskey Jack is never far from another story. The place is both a refuge and a crossroads.
